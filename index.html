About

Oil change was 4,000 km ago? Or was it 6,000? MotoLog is the logbook for riders who'd rather be riding. It remembers what your bike needs, so you don't have to.

Features

Never miss a service

Set intervals for oil, chain, tyres, brake fluid and more, by distance or time. MotoLog flags what's due soon and what's overdue.

02

Your mileage at a glance

Log your odometer after a ride and see distance per month and per year. Service reminders always match how much you actually ride.

FAQ

Questions riders ask

Which bikes does MotoLog work with?

Any motorcycle or scooter. You set the service intervals, so it fits sport bikes, adventure bikes, cruisers and commuters alike.

Can I change the service intervals?

Yes. Edit any interval, add your own jobs, or remove the ones that don't apply to your bike.

Does it work in miles?

Yes, you can choose kilometres or miles.

Can I track more than one bike?

Yes. Each bike in your garage has its own services, mileage and trips.
